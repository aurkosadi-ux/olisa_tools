3The ledger

Dates in a PI are read as day-month-year and written the same way everywhere. Rows are ordered by Pending Days, longest wait first. Every figure is cross-checked against the amount written in words; anything the tool could not read with confidence is marked in red — click any cell to correct it by hand. In the Excel file, Pending Days stays a live =TODAY()-PI Date formula, so the sheet ages itself.
