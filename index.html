Matched Preview

Verdict = the reply after the check, drafted from the records, written from our side (we deliver, Olisa receives). Green = already delivered in full — challan numbers, dates and inventory date are the proof to send back. Amber = genuinely pending (never dispatched, or delivered-but-short so a re-delivery is owed). Pink = style not found in the Master File or PI index — check the code manually. Undelivered Qty = never dispatched; Undelivered Short Qty = delivered on a challan but received short; together they're the total owed. Manual challans always carry the word Manual next to the challan number.
